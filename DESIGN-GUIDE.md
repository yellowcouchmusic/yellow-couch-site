# YCM visual direction — Warm editorial / V2

## Brand character

A music collective and cultural journal: welcoming, rhythmic, curious, artist-first. More independent record shop than generic tech startup. Warmth and personality with enough restraint to let photos and covers lead.

## Color system

| Use | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Site background | Warm paper | `#F7F4EC` |
| Primary text | Near-black ink | `#23241E` |
| Main accent | Couch yellow | `#F0C44B` |
| Secondary accent | Brick/terracotta | `#AF6952` |
| Supporting accent | Moss green | `#5B6C60` |
| Divider lines | Faded paper | `#D8D3C6` |

Use yellow mostly for the album, key buttons, the monogram, and occasional highlights rather than saturating every surface.

## Typography

- Hero, navigation, section headings: **Space Grotesk**, weight 700 for expressive, sturdy lettering.
- Body copy and journal excerpts: **DM Sans**, typically 15–16px, generous line height.
- Editorial italic moments, article titles, album name: **Instrument Serif**.
- Dates, metadata, section labels: **DM Mono**.

Fonts are requested through Google Fonts. Local fallbacks apply offline.

### Photo choices

Favor candid performances, people in conversation, hands/instruments and real event atmosphere. Keep original photo colors unless a subtle warm tone is useful. Avoid heavy Instagram-style filters. Never crop faces or featured instrument details awkwardly. A mix of intimate and wide shots gives the journal editorial rhythm.

### Practical image prep

- JPG/WebP for photos (ideally 300–500 KB per card; album cover can be higher quality). Compress before committing.
- Keep useful alt text in journal; in `content.js`, change the `image` path when adding a new post.
- Use consistent lowercase filenames with hyphens.
- Remember that filenames on GitHub Pages are case-sensitive.
